# Threat Analysis Case Study

## Overview: 
* The study shows a simulated breach and explains the following: 
- What was observed 
- What makes it a threat
- How to measure the threats and respond quickly 
- Possible actions to take after dealing with the threat. 

It covers the step by step guide on basic threat analysis and lists and explains the duties of the threat response team in any given organization. 

I engaged with this project because I have seen many organizations fold over the years after one or a series of cyber attacks. This is because they have been unable to deal with their attackers, and the main cause of this is lack of knowledge of the threats themselves. 

On this repository, you should be able to identify basic organizational threats, their possible causes and probable effects, threat mitigation and response and ultimately, how to keep moving forward after a cyber attack.
